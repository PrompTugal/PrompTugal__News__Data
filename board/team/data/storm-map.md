Notes for reuse:

- No chart on the list needs a CDN library. All are plain SVG or canvas written by the Storm's agents (Apache-2.0). They read colours from design tokens in `app/site.css`, so the data site needs a token mapping; its `src/design/design.css` is the place.
- The two flight maps will not draw from the archive alone. They read `map/snapshots`, `tracks`, `map/wind`, `runways.json` and `movements.json`, which `tools/flights-map.mjs` generates at build time and which are neither in git nor in the 142-file archive (the archive has index.json, crosswind.json, frames, snapshots, summaries and wind). Run `tools/flights-map.mjs` over the archived files, or collect fresh.
- The wind map's `field.json` and `coast.json` (72 KB) are not in the archive: copy them.
- Libraries elsewhere: the graph (`app/graph/engine.js`) loads 3d-force-graph 1.80.1 and three 0.186.1 (1.6 MB) from web-summit.sgit.ai; the Universe brain and live 3D map in `app/agents/vendor/` need the same. Leave them frozen. `tools/broadcast-coast.mjs` fetched world-atlas 2.0.2 from unpkg once, at build time.
- The viz files use the Storm's frame (`frame.js` loads them on a `data-*` mount; some call `stormPanel` for a side panel). Copy with a small loader, and make the panel optional.
- Licences on data shown: adsb.lol ODbL 1.0 (share-alike on our derived snapshots, attribute), Open-Meteo CC BY 4.0, METAR public domain, OurAirports public domain, Natural Earth public domain, INE census and IPMA as stated on their own pages.

## Gaps and risks

- The archive has 142 files and 5.1 MB; the Storm's `data/` folder holds exactly those 142 JSON files (flights 91, weather 12, decisions 22, exposure 7, transport 8, signals 1, watch 1). Everything else in the 2,776 JSON files is not in it.
- Flight map support files, the wind field and the coast file are the three things the front page needs that are not archived.
- Flights are not archived beyond 12 Nov 18:00 UTC frames; the storm's peak has no aircraft positions (adsb.lol's history archive for 11 Nov was expected, not collected).
- OpenSky counts are paused: its terms ask a written licence. Do not restart them.
- The Storm states that its data licence is not distinct from the code's (Apache-2.0) and that Dinis is to confirm; quoted sources keep their own terms.
- Media watch, social and evidence name outlets, accounts and people; carrying them needs consent under the CLAUDE.md rules.
