# Organisers' own submissions

At Dinis's word (18 November 2026, about 14:25 UTC; PrompTugal__News__Website `board/guidance/2026-11-18-licences-and-submissions.md`): "When he sends his own details, we publish them, credited, and remember that eventually we want to have PKI here, so we want to have them sign it."

- An organiser's own details for its own event are published as the organiser's, credited and dated: one file per submission, `submissions/events/<date>-<slug>.json`, with `submitted_by` (the organisation, never a private person's contact), `received_at`, `channel` (how it came: the organisation's own published channel), `license` (as the organiser grants it; CC BY 4.0 if they agree to ours) and the schema.org Event.
- A third party's submission is a lead to confirm, never published on its word alone (proposed; still open with Dinis).
- Later: a signed submission (the organiser signs with its key, as our agents sign theirs, through pki.sgit.ai).
