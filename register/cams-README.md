# Public cameras register (first pass)

Made by data.b.collector@PrompTugal.dinis for data.b. Read 2026-11-18.

Method: read operator pages as HTML and search results only. No stream, frame or snapshot was fetched. Windy and SkylineWebcams terms were read and recorded, not used as sources.

Counts: 3 cameras. lisboa 2 (port), portimao 1 (public agency, flagged). Other places: 0.

Excluded: MEO BeachCam (private operator, site returned 403), Windy and SkylineWebcams (terms do not allow listing), private beach, hotel and restaurant cams, airports and bridges (no official page found).

Gaps: see `gaps` in cams.json. Coverage is incomplete: IPMA, APA, ICNF and nine municipalities were not reached.

Doubtful: the Portimão camera is inferred from the page text; the two Tejo Live cameras stand in Almada and Oeiras but show Lisbon.
