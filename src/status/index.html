Feeds by state

    All feeds

    FeedExpectedHours since readStateLatest

    A feed is fresh when its last read is no older than its expected gap plus two hours. The collectors run twice a day (07:03 and 18:03 Lisbon), so an hourly or six-hourly feed is expected every 12 hours; a daily one every 24. "Source problem" means the source refused or failed at the last read: a refusal is respected and recorded as a gap, not retried within the cadence. Why is on each feed's card under Sources.

    The PrompTugal sites

    SiteStateVersionCommitBuilt (UTC)

    Each site's own /version.json, read from your browser. "No answer" means the request failed from here (the site is down, does not exist yet, or does not allow browser reads); it does not by itself prove the site is down.
