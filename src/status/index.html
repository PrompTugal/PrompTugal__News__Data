    All feeds

    Group by:

    A feed is fresh when its last read is no older than its expected gap plus two hours. The collection runs at 07:03 and 18:03 Lisbon: feeds a card calls hourly or every 6 hours are read at both runs, daily feeds at the first only, so "really collected" can differ from what the card says (the cards still describe an older hourly schedule). "Source problem" means the source refused or failed at the last read; a refusal is respected and recorded as a gap, not retried within the cadence. Files and items are counted at the last wake (...); items are the first count a feed's file reports about itself, so they are not comparable between feeds. Why a feed has a problem is on its card under Sources.
