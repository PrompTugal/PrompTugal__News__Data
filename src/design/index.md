# Three front pages to choose from

PrompTugal.news · Data. The site holds the raw public data behind every PrompTugal site: one collector, one vault, one API and one card per source. No articles. Each variation is live and reads the same JSON. This is a proposal, for Dinis Cruz to choose. HTML version: [index.html](index.html).

## A. The ledger
[Open A alone](a/index.html) · [markdown](a/index.md)

Serves journalists checking a value and anyone who wants the whole picture at a glance. It does well at density: every source on one line with publisher, last read, status, headline count, terms and links, so a claim can be traced in seconds. It does badly at drama and at teaching a newcomer what to do: there is no single live number, and a developer has to find the endpoint in a link.

## B. Now
[Open B alone](b/index.html) · [markdown](b/index.md)

Serves the curious reader and the other PrompTugal sites' editors. It does well at showing that the data is live: warnings by level, stations reporting, airline flights near Faro and fire risk, each with its source and time read. It does badly at breadth and at reference use: it picks four numbers, so a source without a tile is further down, and the terms are not on view.

## C. For builders
[Open C alone](c/index.html) · [markdown](c/index.md)

Serves developers and agents on the other PrompTugal sites. It does well at the first task they have: the stable address, a curl line to copy, and the OpenAPI and Swagger links for each source, with status after. It does badly for everyone else: a journalist or a curious reader sees addresses, not values, and the page says little about what the data means.
